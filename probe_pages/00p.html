    Overview Product

    Meet Your All-New Galaxy Z Fold8

    Discover the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8, a premium foldable smartphone that combines an innovative design, powerful performance, advanced camera capabilities, and intelligent Galaxy AI features. Enjoy a compact cover screen when folded and an immersive main display when unfolded, giving you more freedom to work, create, and enjoy your favorite content.

    Lightweight Design. Limitless Possibilities

    Experience a refined foldable design engineered for comfort and portability. Weighing just 201g and measuring only 4.5mm when unfolded, the Galaxy Z Fold8 is designed to fit comfortably in your hand while delivering an expansive screen experience. Its Armor Aluminum frame, Corning Gorilla Glass, and advanced Armor FlexHinge combine style with everyday durability.

    Two Displays. One Seamless Experience

    Enjoy a 5.5-inch cover display for quick access to your apps, messages, and everyday tasks. Unfold the Galaxy Z Fold8 to reveal a spacious 7.6-inch main display, giving you more room to watch videos, browse the web, read documents, and enjoy immersive entertainment.

    Multitask with More Freedom

    Make the most of the large main display with an optimized multi-window experience. Open multiple apps side by side, review documents while messaging, and switch between tasks with ease. One UI 9 is designed to make your foldable experience more intuitive, flexible, and productive.

    Powerful Performance with Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy

    Powered by the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy processor, the Galaxy Z Fold8 delivers fast, responsive performance for demanding applications, smooth multitasking, immersive gaming, and AI-powered features. Its customized processor combines powerful CPU, GPU, and NPU capabilities to help you get more done throughout your day.

    Capture More with Dual 50MP Cameras

    Capture stunning photos and videos with the 50MP wide and 50MP ultra-wide rear cameras. Enjoy detailed images, expansive landscapes, and group photos with impressive clarity. The 10MP cover camera and 10MP main screen camera provide convenient options for selfies and video calls, while 2x optical-quality zoom helps you capture distant subjects with enhanced detail.

    Brighter Videos with Nightography

    Capture memorable moments even in low-light conditions with Nightography. The 50MP wide camera and ProVisual Engine work together to reduce visual noise and enhance image details, helping you record clearer, more vibrant videos. Record in up to 8K resolution and preserve the moments that matter with impressive clarity.

    Dual Recording. Capture Every Perspective

    Record interviews, reactions, and memorable moments using the front and rear cameras simultaneously with Dual Recording. Capture yourself and your subject in one video, making it easier to create engaging content without additional editing.

    Galaxy AI for Smarter Everyday Experiences

    Discover a more intelligent way to use your smartphone with Galaxy AI. Now Nudge provides helpful suggestions based on what is on your screen, helping you move between tasks more smoothly. With the large foldable display, you can view relevant information and work across apps without interrupting your flow.

    My FanCam. Focus on What Matters

    Turn group videos into personalized, subject-focused clips with My FanCam. Galaxy AI helps you select a person in your video and create a cropped version that keeps the focus on your chosen subject. Adjust the framing and create content that is ready to share directly from your Gallery.

    Photo Assist. Edit Photos with Simple Prompts

    Enhance your photos with Photo Assist and Galaxy AI. Describe the changes you want using simple text prompts, then let AI help transform your images. Move or remove objects, adjust backgrounds, and refine your photos directly on the large main display. Compare the original and edited versions side by side to get the result you want.

    A Battery Built for Your Big Screen

    The 4800mAh battery is designed to keep up with your daily activities, from streaming and gaming to browsing and multitasking. Enjoy up to 26 hours of video playback under specified test conditions, so you can stay entertained and connected for longer.
